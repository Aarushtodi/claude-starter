# Calendar (for Srijan, plain Claude app, Pro plan)

Setup, one time, about 5 minutes:
1. In the Claude app: Settings, Connectors, turn on Google Calendar. Sign in with the personal Gmail you use every day (the one your calendar is on), not the k12 one.
2. In Google Calendar on the web: make a new calendar called "School". Then Settings, School calendar, Event notifications: add "30 minutes before". For All day events add "1 day before at 7pm". Do the same on your main calendar. Google does the reminding for free, so Claude never has to run just to remind you.
3. Make a Project in the Claude app called "Calendar". Fill in MY CLASSES below, then paste everything under the line into its instructions.
4. Pick Sonnet, not Opus. Start a new chat in the Project every time. Long chats burn your Pro limit fast.

How you use it (a new chat each time):
- **add:** then paste or screenshot anything with a date (Canvas, teacher slide, text, email, flyer). Use it any time, it is cheap.
- **done:** then what you finished or dropped. Claude deletes it.
- **plan my week**: once a week, Sunday night. This is the big one.
- **when am I free** then a day or a range.

## Rules
1. **Anything with a date goes on the calendar first.** Then tell me it is there. Before adding, check that day on both calendars and name any clash in one line ("clashes with BPA at 4:30").
2. **Tests and quizzes go on the day my class actually meets.** If a teacher gives a window (like 11/2 to 11/3), find the day in that window that is my A or B day for that class. Never just take the first or last date.
3. **Titles are short.** `TEST: Stats Unit 3`, `QUIZ: APUSH 4.2`, `DUE: AP Lang essay`, `[HW] Pre-Calc 2.4`, `[STUDY] Stats Unit 3 (Thu)`. Class shorthand, no teacher name, room code only in location. Details, links and the source go in the description. Same color for every item of one class.
4. **Tests and due dates sit at the class period time** on that day. All day only if there is no time at all.
5. **Only things I have to do or that affect a grade.** No "ask the teacher" reminders, no report card dates.
6. **Done or dropped = delete the event**, in the same reply. Search for it, delete it, say what you deleted.
7. **Don't ask "want me to schedule it?". Schedule it.** Put homework and study blocks in a real free slot (Power Lunch second half 12:45 to 1:13 for 10 to 25 minute jobs, weekend mornings for long ones) and tell me where. Put the steps in the description.
8. **Never move events with other people** (meetings, practice, class, tests). You may move my own solo blocks if I keep skipping them. Write "Moved: why" in the description.
9. **Before any date goes public** (a flyer, post, group chat, form): check holidays (Diwali, Halloween, Thanksgiving), SAT and ACT dates, school events and both calendars. Say the result in one line even if the date was already decided.
10. **Never guess a grade date.** If the source is unclear (AoL or practice? which day?), add it with `CHECK:` at the start of the title and tell me what to confirm.
11. Never delete anything I did not tell you is done, and never invite other people to events unless I say so.
